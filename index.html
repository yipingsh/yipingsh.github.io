A bit about me

I'm pursuing a BSc in Information and Communication Technology at the Singapore University of Social Sciences, after completing a Diploma in Information Technology at Singapore Polytechnic. I'm most interested in where software development meets AI, and I enjoy building things end to end, from the database to the interface.

I'm now looking for an internship in software development or applied AI, where I can contribute to real products and keep learning from experienced engineers. Outside of tech, I enjoy hiking and rock climbing.
